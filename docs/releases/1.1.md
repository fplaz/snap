# Snap 1.1

- Drag selection borders to move the screenshot area and annotations together.
- Resize from the bottom-right corner; annotations stay in place and are clipped to the canvas when exported.
- Toolbar follows the bottom and right edges while resizing, with its right edge attached to the canvas.
- Rectangle is the default tool. Menu-bar icon includes an S.
- Uploads show the mini progress panel without bringing My Screenshots forward.
- Custom dark metallic drag-to-install DMG with a Retina background and fixed icon positions.

Quit Snap before replacing it in Applications. This release uses the existing self-signed identity and file-based Keychain. It remains unnotarized.
