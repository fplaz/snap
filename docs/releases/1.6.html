Snap 1.6

  • Snap sets itself to open at login the first time you launch it from Applications. Manage it in System Settings → General → Login Items & Extensions.
  • The ⌘⇧6 shortcut has been removed. Choose Take screenshot and copy from the Snap menu for a quick copy, or press ⌘⇧7 to capture and annotate.
  • While editing text, press Tab to finish editing and switch to the next tool, or Shift+Tab to switch to the previous tool. Both work in the capture overlay and the pop-out editor.
  • Start drawing closer to the screenshot edge with a narrower drag zone for moving the selection.
